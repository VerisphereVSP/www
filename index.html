How the protocol works

1

Post a claim

A claim is a public, checkable statement, registered on-chain with an open record of who said it and when.

2

Stake on accuracy

Holders stake VSP to support or challenge the claim. Stake size and timing set each side's position — conviction has a cost.

3

Settle

At settlement, the accurate side earns from the inaccurate side under fixed, audited rules. No committee, no moderator — economics does the arguing.
