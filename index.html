How the protocol works

1

Post a claim

A claim is a public, checkable statement, registered on-chain with an open record of who said it and when.

2

Stake on accuracy

Holders stake VSP to support or challenge the claim. Stake size and timing set each side's position — conviction has a cost.

3

Earn or pay

Each epoch, accurate stakes gain and inaccurate stakes lose under fixed on-chain rules — every position on its own terms, independent of the other side. Claims never close — new evidence can always move the score. No committee, no moderator — economics does the arguing.
